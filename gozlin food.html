01 / ABOUT US

From the farm,
with purpose.

Gozlin Foods is a customer-focused yam brand created around a simple idea: quality food should be easier to find, easier to trust and easier to order. We specialize in supplying yam for everyday cooking and larger food needs, with attention to selection, freshness, cleanliness and dependable service.

Whether you are preparing a family meal, running a restaurant, catering an event or stocking a food shop, Gozlin Foods aims to make yam supply simple. We believe every tuber should represent value—from the moment it is selected to the moment it reaches the customer.

Our brand stands for consistency, honesty and a passion for one of the foods that remains important in many African kitchens. Gozlin Foods is more than selling yam; it is about building a dependable food brand people can return to whenever they need quality produce.
